## 4. Commercial Calculation Protection & Invariance Verification

The calculation engine's commercial logic in [`src/calculation-engine/modules/budget.ts`](file:///c:/Users/Avita/Desktop/Programming/big%20bnglore%20client/cost%20calculator%20rightcon/src/calculation-engine/modules/budget.ts#L100-L160) and [`src/store/useWizardStore.ts`](file:///c:/Users/Avita/Desktop/Programming/big%20bnglore%20client/cost%20calculator%20rightcon/src/store/useWizardStore.ts#L249-L253) was verified for strict invariance:

### Existing Production Defaults:
- **`contractorMode`:** Defaults to `'independent'` (Self-build / direct labour management).
- **`contractorMarginRate`:** Defaults to `0.10` (**10%**). Slider range is configured from 8% to 20%.
- **`professionalFees`:** Defaults to `0.00` (**0%** in independent mode; configurable up to 5%).
- **`applyContractorGST`:** Defaults to `false` (Opt-in toggle for 18% GST).

### Mathematical Invariance Guarantee:
- **Base Works BOQ Invariance:** Direct Construction Cost ($\sum \text{BOQ Items} = \text{Materials} + \text{Labour} + \text{Fixtures}$) is completely independent of commercial toggles.
- **Brand Invariance:** Selecting UltraTech vs ACC Cement, or Tata Tiscon vs Jindal Steel, adjusts the unit rate and line cost, but **never alters the physical quantity** of steel (kg), cement (bags), or concrete ($m^3$).
- **Zero-Residual Check:** Total project cost equals the exact sum of direct costs, contractor margin, contingency, fees, and GST:
  $$\text{Total Project Cost} \equiv C_{\text{direct}} + M_{\text{contractor}} + R_{\text{contingency}} + F_{\text{prof}} + \text{GST} \quad (\text{Residual} \equiv 0.00)$$
