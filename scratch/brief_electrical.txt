Hutty Electrical — UI & Final Report
Developer Implementation Guide
This document defines what the customer should see for the Electrical section in Hutty, both in the interactive calculator UI
and in the final BOQ/report. The calculation engine should remain point-driven and transparent, while the customer-facing
presentation stays simple.
1. Customer Experience Principle
Do not expose the engineering complexity by default. The customer should immediately understand what electrical
work is included, how much material is estimated, what specification/brand is selected, and what it costs.
Primary customer questions should be answered in this order:
• What am I installing? — electrical points, switches/sockets, DBs and appliance provisions.
• What am I consuming? — wiring, conduit and other electrical materials.
• What does it cost? — quantity × rate, with an electrical subtotal.
• How was it calculated? — optional transparency layer showing the assumptions behind the estimate.

Provide a Change Specification action. Changing Anchor to another available brand/specification should update the
product specification and rate. It should not arbitrarily change the calculated physical quantity.

8. Final Report — Electrical Section
The final PDF/BOQ should preserve the same structure used in the interactive UI, but can be more detailed.
• Electrical Summary: total electrical cost, selected brand/specification and effective cost/sq.ft where appropriate.
• What We Install: light points, fan points, switches/sockets, AC points, geyser points, TV/data, DBs and selected
appliance provisions.
• What We Consume: wire by size, conduit, boxes/accessories and other priced electrical materials.
• What It Costs: item, quantity, unit, rate and amount, followed by the electrical subtotal.
• Calculation Notes: concise explanation of the assumptions and the fact that the result is a preliminary estimate.

How is this calculated?
Hutty estimates electrical requirements from your selected rooms, floors and appliance requirements, using preliminary
routing and quantity parameters.
12. Do Not Show by Default
• Voltage-drop calculations
• Detailed current/load calculations
• Breaker sizing calculations
• Circuit loading schedules
• Phase balancing
• Internal route assumptions and engineering formulas
• 10% wastage arithmetic
These may exist in the calculation engine or technical view, but should not clutter the primary customer report.
13. Engineering / Estimation Disclaimer
Hutty's electrical quantities are preliminary estimation quantities. They are not a substitute for final electrical design. Final conductor sizing, circuit
loading, voltage-drop checks, protection, DB design and installation details must be verified by a qualified electrical designer/engineer for the
actual project.
14. Developer Acceptance Checklist
I Electrical quantities are generated from the user's actual house configuration.
I No universal fixed 700 m rule is used.
I Wire is clearly identified as conductor length or cable route length.
I Wire size quantities are separately displayed where applicable.
I Conduit quantity is separately calculated.
