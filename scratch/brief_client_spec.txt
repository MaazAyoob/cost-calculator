Purpose. This document explains how Hutty converts homeowner inputs into building geometry, physical quantities,
selected-material costs, BOQ schedules and a final project estimate. The formulas and rates below are reproduced from
the specifications supplied for this project; assumptions and commercial parameters remain subject to client/Rightcon
confirmation.
1. HOW THE ENGINE WORKS
Hutty follows a forward calculation chain. The homeowner does not enter raw civil quantities. Instead, design inputs drive
a canonical space model, which produces quantities. Material choices then supply rates for those quantities.
USER INPUTS → SPACE MODEL → GEOMETRY → PHYSICAL QUANTITIES → MATERIAL SELECTION → UNIT RATES →
BOQ → TOTAL COST

2. STEP-BY-STEP USER INPUT MODEL
Step 1 — Site & Building Inputs. Location, plot dimensions, setback rules, number of floors/storeys, house type and
parking type are collected. These inputs determine the buildable footprint and built-up area.
Step 2 — House Configuration. Bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen/dining, living, balcony/utility and related spaces drive
masonry, doors/windows, electrical, plumbing, flooring, paint and fixture quantities.
Steps 3–10 — Material Selection. The homeowner selects preferred materials/brands. These selections change the
applicable unit rates and therefore cost, while the underlying physical quantity remains governed by the design and
quantity formulas.
Final output. Hutty presents: (1) Works BOQ, (2) Material Schedule, (3) Fixtures & Fittings Schedule, and (4) Cost
Summary.

11. SECTION D — COMMERCIAL COST ENGINE
The supplied specification defines the following cost aggregation. The base cost is derived from the Works BOQ, then
commercial/statutory additions are calculated from the stated bases.
Base Construction Cost = Σ (Section A Works BOQ line items)
Professional Fees = Base Construction Cost × 5%
Contractor Margin = Base Construction Cost × 15%
Contingency Reserve = Base Construction Cost × 6%
GST = (Base Cost + Professional Fees) × 18%
Total Project Cost = Base Cost + Professional Fees + Margin + Contingency + GST
Effective Rate / Sq.Ft = Total Project Cost ÷ Total Built-Up Area

17. ZERO STATE & LIVE PREVIEW
At the beginning of a new calculation, the engine should remain honest and empty: Plot = 0, BUA = 0, Cost = I0 and no
material/space quantities until the user provides the required inputs. As inputs change, the live preview consumes the
same calculation result used by the report.

19. ENGINEERING TRACEABILITY PRINCIPLE
Every quantity should be traceable to an input, an assumption/rule, a formula, a unit and a resulting value. Every cost
should be traceable to quantity × unit rate. This makes the calculator auditable rather than a black-box estimate.
