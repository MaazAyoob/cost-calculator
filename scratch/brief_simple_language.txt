3. Homepage wording
Main heading
Understand your home before you build.
Supporting text
Plan your house, see how much it may cost, understand the materials you may need, and get help from experts when you need
it.
Main buttons
• Start Free Estimate
• Consult an Expert — I1,499
Avoid words that sound too technical or promise too much
• Avoid “zero guesswork”, “calculated to the inch” or “verified quantities” unless the team can prove these claims.
• Replace “bank-ready” with “A detailed report you can discuss with your bank or contractor.” Do not promise a bank will accept it.
• Replace “Your contractor is oversmarting you?” with “Not sure about your contractor’s quote? Check the work and costs, or speak
to an expert.”
• Support the “25%–40% cost overruns” claim with a reliable source or remove it.
4. Calculator and report
• Explain what each plan includes and what the customer gets after paying.
• Use clear labels: Materials, Labour, Other Costs, Total Estimated Cost and Payment Plan.
• Explain that prices can change by location, material choice, design and site conditions.
• Show when rates were last checked, or say they are estimates if they are not checked regularly.
• Make sure the figures on the website and in the downloaded report match.
Show this note near the estimate and in the PDF:
This is an early estimate to help you plan. Final costs and quantities may change after the site, drawings
and material choices are checked.
5. Consultation page
Suggested heading
Need help building your home? Talk to an expert.
Supporting text
Get help with your house plan, contractor quote, materials, structure or site questions. Not sure who you need? Tell us about
your problem and Hutty will help you find the right person.
Show four simple choices
• Check my contractor’s quote
• Find an architect or engineer
• Get help with a problem at my site
• I’m not sure — help me choose
Booking steps
• Ask the customer to describe their question, share their location and choose a suitable date and time.
• Show the expert’s name, experience, price, session length and what the customer will receive.
• After booking, show a confirmation and tell the customer what happens next.
• If no expert is available, let the customer send a request and tell them when Hutty will respond.

6. Important checks before launch
• I All prices are correct: Calculator Free / I499 / I1,499; Consultation I1,499.
• I Old I99 price is removed everywhere.
• I The three calculator plans clearly explain what the customer gets.
• I The I1,499 calculator plan and I1,499 consultation look like separate services.
• I The same project shows the same total on the website and in the PDF.
• I Material quantities and costs have been checked by a construction professional.
• I The estimate note is visible before and after downloading the report.
• I The calculator and report work on mobile phones.
• I Every consultation request reaches the Hutty team and gets a response.
• I Every expert shown is real, and their details have been checked.
• I Payment success, payment failure and booking confirmation have been tested.
• I Contact details, privacy information and cancellation/refund terms are easy to find.
7. Developer instructions
• Keep the current design where it works. Make the changes above instead of rebuilding the whole website.
• Use everyday words that a first-time homeowner can understand. If a technical term is needed, explain it briefly.
• Fix pricing and calculation problems before adding new features.
• Test the full journey on desktop and mobile, then share the updated website link and a list of anything still pending.
DONE WHEN
A homeowner can understand the website, create an estimate, understand the
report and either book an expert or ask Hutty for help—without needing
construction knowledge.
